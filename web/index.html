Gmail

Jarvis can read, search, send and reply to email. Every email he writes is shown to you first and only goes out when you click Send. He can't delete mail or mark it as read.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account.
  2. Open App passwords, name it Jarvis and click Create.
  3. Paste the 16-letter password below and save.

Google Calendar

Lets Jarvis read your calendar for "what's on today" and briefings. New events open in Google Calendar ready to save.

  1. Open Google Calendar settings and click your calendar under "Settings for my calendars".
  2. Scroll to "Integrate calendar" and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  3. Paste it below and save. Anyone with this address can see your events, so keep it private.

Picture generation (Cloudflare, free)

Lets Jarvis make pictures when you say "draw…" or "generate…". Cloudflare's free plan includes a daily allowance of AI pictures.

  1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com and log in.
  2. In the left menu open AI → Workers AI, then click Use REST API.
  3. Click Create a Workers AI API Token, then Create API Token, and copy the token.
  4. On the same page copy your Account ID. Paste both below and save.

Phone

Two ways to have Jarvis on your phone. The phone app works on its own, even when this PC is off.

Phone app (works when the PC is off)

Your phone gets its own Jarvis (same Gemini brain and voice). Your profile, tasks, skills and conversation sync through a private GitHub repo, encrypted with a passphrase only you know, so GitHub only ever sees scrambled data. When this PC is on, the phone can also ask it to do PC things.

Checking…
  1. On GitHub, create a new private repository named jarvis-sync (tick "Add a README").
  2. Create a fine-grained token: name it Jarvis sync, Repository access Only select repositories → jarvis-sync, Permissions Contents → Read and write. Generate and copy it.
  3. Paste it below, choose a passphrase (a short sentence you'll remember: it can't be recovered), and click Turn on.
  1. On your iPhone, open the Camera and scan this code. Jarvis opens in Safari.
  2. Type your passphrase and tap Connect.
  3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Jarvis now has his own icon.

The code holds the repo and its key, never your passphrase. Keep it private.
